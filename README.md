# 🦾 Robot Dog Mechanical Design

> Initial mechanical design of a simple quadruped robot developed using **Tinkercad**.

---

## 📖 Overview

This project presents the initial mechanical design of a four-legged (quadruped) robot dog created in **Tinkercad**. The project focuses on the robot's mechanical structure, joint arrangement, actuator selection, stability analysis, and basic locomotion concept before moving to electronics and programming.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Design the robot body (chassis)
- Design four mechanical legs
- Define joints and Degrees of Freedom (DOF)
- Select suitable servo motors
- Perform an initial torque estimation
- Analyze stability and center of gravity
- Propose a basic walking gait
- Identify potential mechanical limitations and improvements

## ⚙️ Mechanical Design

The robot consists of:

- 🦴 Main body (Chassis)
- 🦿 Four identical legs
- 🔩 Rotational joints
- ⚙️ Servo motor mounting positions

The design emphasizes simplicity, lightweight construction, and ease of future assembly.

---

## 🤖 Servo Motor Selection

The robot is designed to use standard hobby servo motors for leg actuation.

### Considerations

- Required torque
- Weight of each leg
- Ease of control
- Availability
- Cost

---

## 📐 Initial Torque Calculation

The required servo torque was estimated based on:

- Robot body weight
- Leg length
- Distance from the joint to the center of mass

This preliminary calculation helps determine whether the selected servo motors can safely support and move the robot.

---

## ⚖️ Stability Analysis

The design considers:

- Low center of gravity
- Symmetrical leg placement
- Balanced weight distribution
- Stable support polygon during standing

These factors improve the robot's overall stability during movement.

---

## 🚶 Proposed Walking Method

The robot is intended to use a simple quadruped gait.

Basic walking sequence:

1. Lift one leg
2. Move it forward
3. Place it on the ground
4. Repeat the sequence for the remaining legs

This concept can later be implemented using servo control algorithms.

---

## ⚠️ Potential Mechanical Challenges

- Servo torque limitations
- Joint flexibility
- Structural rigidity
- Weight distribution
- Walking stability
- Material strength

Future iterations can improve these aspects through mechanical optimization.
